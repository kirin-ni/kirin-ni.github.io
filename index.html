Kirin Personal Drive Bridge

This site provides information about a private tool used by its owner to access and manage the owner's own Google Drive files through Google APIs.

The tool is intended for personal workflows such as file listing, transfer, synchronization, and management initiated by the owner. It is not offered as a public service and does not provide public user registration.

Google account access is granted by the account owner through Google's authorization flow and can be revoked from the Google Account security settings.
