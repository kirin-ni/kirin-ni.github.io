Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy describes how the owner-operated, personal-use Google Drive integration handles Google user data. The application is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

After the owner explicitly authorizes access, the application may access Google Drive data permitted by the OAuth scopes selected for the integration. The actual data available depends on those permissions and the actions the owner requests.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to carry out the owner's requested Google Drive functions, such as listing, organizing, transferring, synchronizing, or managing files. The application does not use Google user data for advertising or sell it.

Processing and sharing

Data may be processed on systems controlled by the owner and, when the owner configures a transfer or synchronization destination, sent to that destination to complete the requested operation. The application does not disclose Google user data to third parties for their independent advertising or marketing. The owner is responsible for the security and privacy practices of any separately configured destination or service.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens and other configuration data may be stored by the owner's client or server so that authorized tasks can run. They should be protected against unauthorized access. This website itself does not provide a login form, collect Google Drive files, or run analytics or advertising trackers.

Retention and revocation

Data and tokens are retained only as needed for the owner's personal workflows and may be deleted from the systems where they are stored. The owner can revoke the application's Google access through their Google Account security settings. Revoking access may stop integrations that depend on it.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the application's behavior changes. This is a personal project, not a public service.
